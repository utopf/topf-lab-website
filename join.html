PhD Student Positions

Our PhD students are part of the
The Doctoral School of Molecular Biology and Biological Chemistry of IBB PAS. Admissions to the Doctoral School take place twice a year, in March and October. Check here for open positions.

Postdoctoral Positions

We currently don`t have funded postions for Postdoctoral Researchers. If you share our passion about proteostasis regulation during aging we are happy to explore potential research projects and funding opportunities, including postdoctoral fellowships from NAWA, EMBO, and MSCA.

While our research is largely driven by experimental wet-lab work, we are particularly enthusiastic about candidates who are interested in integrating proteomics data analysis and/or structural approaches into their research. Get in contact and send your CV and a brief summary of your research interests.

Bachelor/Master/Interships

Interested in molecular biology, cell biology, biochemistry? We offer Bachelor and Master thesis projects.

We welcome enthusiastic students for research internships who wish to gain hands-on experience in the wet-lab and learn more about the work we do in our lab.

For informal inquiries, send an email to Ulrike (utopf@ibb.waw.pl)
